SUDOKU ARCADE

Privacy policy · Effective 8 November 2026

Sudoku Arcade is an Android puzzle game published on Google Play by Information Technology Systems ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Sudoku Arcade does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.

Information stored on your device

To let you resume puzzles and see your progress, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • the puzzle in progress
  • your game results, scores and statistics
  • your settings, such as theme, reduce motion and celebrations

This information never leaves the app. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup, under your own Google account and subject to Google's privacy policy. We do not have access to these backups. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Permissions

Sudoku Arcade does not request any runtime permissions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
